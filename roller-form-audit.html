Worth acting on

/get-started/ runs two forms, neither of them a normal embed. “2023 - Let’s chat v2” renders into #hubspot-form-wrapper via an inline hbspt.forms.create() call, then reveals the Calendly widget with name and email prefilled. When Calendly fires calendly.event_scheduled, the page POSTs directly to the HubSpot Forms API to submit “Calendly tag registration form” with calendly_booked=true and the event and invitee URIs. That second form has no markup on the page at all.

The “Calendly tag registration form” conversion figures are meaningless. It shows 4 page views against 211 submissions because it never renders — HubSpot’s submissions-per-page-view of 5,275 is an artefact. Treat those 211 as a count of completed Calendly bookings.

“Let’s chat v2” is shared between /get-started/ and /iaapa-europe-2026, so its 8,748 submissions cannot be split by form ID. On the IAAPA page it is embedded twice — once through the standard module and once into a hidden #footerHSForm div — worth confirming that is intentional and not double-firing.

A form named “2023 - Get Started (Spam Leads)” is live on two solutions pages with 36 submissions, last one 3 August 2026. Separately, “2023 - Get Started” (770 submissions) sits on /solutions/enterprise, not on /get-started/. The naming across those three needs untangling.

Seven forms have not been submitted in over six months while their pages stay published and in the sitemap. The Guest Experience Playbook form has been silent since September 2022, nearly four years.

6 forms sit on more than one page, so their submissions pool and cannot be attributed by form ID. For per-page numbers on any of them you need HubSpot’s page-level breakdown.

No literal <form> tags exist in the served HTML — every form on roller.software is injected by JavaScript. Detection covered three mechanisms: the standard module options block, inline hbspt.forms.create() calls, and direct POSTs to api.hsforms.com/submissions/v3. Each embed's target div was located to determine whether it sits inside the site footer (<footer id="js-footer">) or the page body; the footer subscribe form is excluded site-wide except where it is separately embedded in the body. Submissions, views and dates are per form and lifetime-to-date, so for a form on several pages they cover all of them together. Your HubSpot export lists 134 forms in total; the 24 here are the ones actually placed on a non-blog page.
